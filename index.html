How a visit works

  1. Text or call with your ZIP code to check availability. To confirm your appointment, you’ll accept the service agreement electronically, pay a $50 deposit, and provide a card through our secure payment processor before dispatch.
  2. Have everyone in your household available for screening if you’d like to qualify for the seven-day limited guarantee.
  3. We recommend treatment for anyone we find has active head lice and provide written aftercare instructions.

Seven-day limited guarantee

If you find a live crawling head louse within seven calendar days, call or text us within that period. Eligible households receive one complimentary follow-up inspection and one complimentary corrective treatment if we confirm live head lice.

Eligibility depends on household screening, completion of recommended treatment, and following aftercare instructions. Read the full guarantee conditions.

Pricing

$150 per hour

Screening and treatment both count toward billable time. There’s a one-hour minimum once service begins. After the first hour, additional time is billed in 15-minute increments at $37.50 per increment, rounded up to the next increment.

$50 booking deposit

The deposit confirms your appointment and is credited toward your final bill once service begins.

A screening-only visit completed within an hour costs $150, with $100 due after the deposit. A three-hour visit costs $450, with $400 due after the deposit. Both examples are before any separately approved charges.

You can set a spending limit before service begins.

Service area

We serve Austin, Westlake, Bee Cave, Driftwood, Dripping Springs, and Lakeway.

Appointments outside this area may be available. We’ll disclose any travel surcharge for your approval before booking. Text us your ZIP code to check.
