I’m Skyler Passino, founder of Clear Sky Lice Relief. I was born in Austin, raised in Dripping Springs, and I’m a dad of two.

What happens at a visit

We start by screening for head lice. If we find lice, we’ll explain what we found and recommend treatment. Treatment involves manually combing out lice and eggs. We’ll leave you with written aftercare instructions and explain how to reach us if you find live lice again.
