1. Parties and Agreement

This Client Service Agreement (“Agreement”) is between Barton Creek Family Services LLC, doing business as Clear Sky Lice Relief (“Company,” “we,” or “us”), and the individual booking and accepting responsibility for payment (“Client” or “you”).

By electronically signing this Agreement, Client acknowledges having read and understood its terms and agrees to be bound by them, subject to applicable law.

The individual booking service agrees to pay the charges properly incurred for the appointment, including authorized services provided to other household members.

2. Nature and Scope of Services

Clear Sky Lice Relief provides mobile, in-home head lice screening and manual lice and nit removal services.

Services may include:

  • Inspection of hair and scalp for evidence of head lice.
  • Sectioning, detangling, and preparation of hair.
  • Application of approved conditioners, olive oil, or other suitable combing aids.
  • Manual removal of visible lice and accessible nits using specialized combs.
  • Reinspection and quality checks.
  • Client education and written aftercare instructions.

Combing aids are used to facilitate removal and are not represented as proven lice-killing medications.

The Company does not provide medical diagnosis, prescribe medications, or guarantee that every louse or nit will be removed during a single appointment.

3. Service Area and Travel

The Company's standard service area includes Austin, Westlake, Bee Cave, Driftwood, Dripping Springs, and Lakeway, Texas.

Appointments outside the standard service area may be accepted at the Company's discretion. Any additional travel surcharge must be disclosed to and approved by Client before booking.

Ordinary travel and parking within the standard service area are included in standard pricing. Unusual parking expenses may be passed through at actual cost only with Client's advance approval.

4. Pricing and Billable Time

The Company's standard rate is $150 per hour, subject to a one-hour minimum charge of $150 once service begins.

After the first hour, time is billed in 15-minute increments at $37.50 per increment. Any partially completed 15-minute increment is billed as a full increment.

Billable services include consultations, household screening, hair preparation, detangling, sectioning, product application, manual removal, reinspection, cleanup, aftercare education, and other activities reasonably necessary to perform the appointment.

The billable clock begins when the technician begins providing services, including initial consultation or inspection, or when authorized client-caused waiting time begins under Section 8.

Time spent traveling to the appointment and delays caused by the Company are not billed at the hourly service rate.

Any advance estimate of appointment duration is informational only and is not a fixed-price quotation.

5. Client Spending Limits

Client may establish a maximum authorized appointment charge before service begins, provided the limit is not less than the applicable one-hour minimum.

The technician must obtain Client's express authorization before exceeding that limit. Any increased limit will be documented.

Client may decline additional time. The technician will then stop at the authorized limit, explain any unfinished work, and document the treatment status.

An incomplete treatment resulting from a spending limit may affect eligibility for the limited guarantee described in Section 17.

6. Booking, Deposit, and Payment Authorization

A $50 deposit is required to confirm an appointment and will be credited toward the final invoice if service begins.

Before dispatch, Client must electronically accept this Agreement, successfully pay the deposit, and provide a valid credit or debit card through the Company's secure payment processor.

Client authorizes the Company to charge the deposit and the properly incurred, authorized final invoice balance to the payment method on file, including any separately approved additional charges, subject to the agreed spending limit.

The final invoice will identify billable time, applicable rates, deposit credit, approved additional charges, and remaining balance.

Payment is due immediately upon completion of service, before the technician departs. Payment is accepted by credit or debit card only.

The Company will provide an electronic receipt.

The Company will not independently store complete payment-card numbers or card security codes.

7. Cancellation and Rescheduling

If Client cancels at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment, the $50 deposit will be refunded.

If Client cancels less than 24 hours before the appointment, the deposit is nonrefundable, except where applicable law or another provision of this Agreement requires otherwise.

If the technician has been dispatched and Client cancels, is absent, denies entry, provides an incorrect address, or prevents service from beginning for reasons within Client's control, the Company may retain the $50 deposit but will assess no additional failed-appointment charge.

If the Company cancels or cannot provide the scheduled appointment, Client may choose a full deposit refund or voluntarily agree to reschedule. Rescheduling is never mandatory.

Any exception to these provisions requires management approval.

8. Arrival, Waiting, and No-Shows

If the technician arrives on time and is ready to begin, Client receives a complimentary 10-minute grace period for client-caused delays before service begins.

If Client is present but not ready after that period, additional waiting time may be billed at the standard hourly rate in 15-minute increments, subject to the agreed spending limit.

If Client is absent or unresponsive, the technician will attempt contact upon arrival and again after approximately 10 minutes.

After 15 minutes total, the technician may classify the appointment as a no-show and depart. The deposit will be forfeited, with no additional charge.

If service never begins because Client cannot provide access or suitable conditions, only the deposit may be retained under the applicable cancellation provisions.

Company-caused delays are not billable.

9. Household Screening and Treatment

To qualify for the Company's seven-day limited guarantee, all household residents must be made available for screening during the initial appointment.

Household residents found to have active head lice should receive the Company's recommended treatment during the initial appointment when reasonably possible.

No person will be forced to undergo screening or treatment.

If a household resident is unavailable, declines screening, or is identified as having an active infestation but does not receive the recommended treatment, the household will not qualify for the seven-day guarantee.

A resident who is screened and found not to have an active infestation does not require treatment merely to preserve guarantee eligibility.

All screening and treatment time is billable under Section 4.

10. Consent and Treatment of Minors

Every adult receiving screening or treatment must personally consent.

A parent or legal guardian must authorize screening and treatment for a minor and remain physically present throughout the minor's appointment.

The responsible adult must supervise the child and assist the technician when reasonably requested. Technicians do not provide childcare or restrain unwilling clients.

The booking Client represents that they have authority to arrange access to the premises and, where applicable, to authorize services for minors in their care.

A technician may decline treatment when consent or legal authority is unclear.

Consent may be withdrawn at any time. Properly incurred charges remain payable.

11. Client Disclosures

Client agrees to disclose, to the best of their knowledge, information reasonably relevant to safe and effective service, including:

  • Household members requiring screening.
  • Known infestations and relevant prior treatment.
  • Allergies, sensitivities, scalp conditions, wounds, or prior adverse reactions.
  • Hair characteristics that may materially affect service duration.
  • Relevant safety hazards, access restrictions, and unsuitable working conditions.

Client agrees to promptly correct materially inaccurate information.

An innocent error will not automatically void the limited guarantee. A material omission or misrepresentation may affect eligibility when it substantially interferes with treatment, safety, or compliance with guarantee conditions.

12. Products and Client-Supplied Conditioner

The Company will identify proposed products and obtain consent before application.

Client may request use of their own suitable conditioner as a combing aid. The product must be in its original, clearly labeled container, intended for human hair or scalp, and reasonably suitable for the procedure.

Technicians may reject homemade, unlabeled, decanted, contaminated, expired, improperly stored, or otherwise unsuitable products.

No additional product or substitution will be applied without authorization.

Clients must disclose known allergies and sensitivities. The Company cannot guarantee that a product will not cause irritation or an allergic reaction.

Client-supplied products do not change the hourly service rate.

13. Hair Preparation and Treatment Limitations

Client should brush and detangle hair before the appointment when reasonably possible.

Any detangling performed by the technician is billable.

Technicians are not required to cut or shave hair, perform chemical treatments, or work through severely matted or damaged hair when doing so could cause unreasonable discomfort or injury.

Technicians may pause, modify, decline, or discontinue treatment when it cannot be performed safely.

Client must secure pets, firearms and other dangerous items, protect valuable or delicate property, and identify materials or surfaces susceptible to damage from water, conditioner, or oil.

Technicians may enter only areas reasonably necessary to perform the agreed services. They will not search unrelated private areas.

16. Informed Consent and Risks

Manual lice removal involves close contact with the hair and scalp and may cause temporary tenderness, irritation, tangling, hair breakage, minor scratches, or reactions to approved combing aids.

Client acknowledges these foreseeable risks and authorizes the agreed procedure.

Technicians must exercise reasonable care and discontinue treatment when continuing could reasonably worsen a suspected injury or adverse reaction.

Technicians do not diagnose scalp conditions or provide medical treatment. Clients with suspected infections, significant inflammation, open wounds, or other medical concerns may be referred to a qualified healthcare professional.

If a previously disclosed condition prevents service from safely beginning despite the Company having accepted the booking, the deposit will be refunded.

This acknowledgment does not release the Company from legally actionable negligence, misconduct, or nonwaivable obligations.

17. Seven-Day Limited Retreatment Guarantee

The Company offers a one-time complimentary follow-up inspection and, when warranted, one complimentary corrective treatment under the conditions below.

If a live crawling head louse is discovered within seven calendar days after the original appointment, Client must notify the Company by phone or text within that period.

The Company will arrange a complimentary follow-up inspection at the earliest mutually available appointment, which may occur after the seventh day if notice was timely.

If the technician confirms live head lice, the Company will provide one complimentary corrective treatment.

Eligibility requires that:

  1. All household residents were made available for initial screening.
  2. Household members identified with active lice received the recommended treatment during the initial appointment.
  3. The original treatment was not left materially incomplete because Client declined necessary services or imposed a spending limit.
  4. Client followed the Company's aftercare instructions, including appropriate monitoring and combing every two to three days.
  5. Client reported the suspected recurrence within seven calendar days.

The Company will provide a Nit Free Terminator comb when Client does not already possess a suitable lice comb.

The guarantee applies whether the confirmed lice originated from organisms remaining after the initial visit or a subsequent exposure. The Company will not require proof of the source of reinfestation.

Itching, scalp irritation, isolated nits, or empty egg casings alone do not establish eligibility for corrective treatment.

The guarantee may be used only once per original appointment. It does not renew after complimentary treatment.

This guarantee does not promise permanent eradication or protection from future exposure and does not limit rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

18. Aftercare Responsibilities

Client agrees to review and follow the Company's written aftercare instructions, including regular combing and household monitoring.

Client understands that additional lice may become apparent after treatment and that repeated monitoring is important.

Optional follow-up services not covered by the limited guarantee are available at the Company's standard rates.

Records and photographs may be retained longer when reasonably necessary for an active complaint, insurance matter, payment dispute, legal obligation, or anticipated litigation.

Records will be stored in company-controlled systems with access limited to authorized personnel.

The Company will not use identifiable client information or treatment photographs for marketing without separate, express authorization.

Declining photographs does not prevent service or invalidate the guarantee.
